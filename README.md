One line, in the dog's own voice, explaining why the homework got eaten — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The line

> At 7:40 this morning, Maya's maths homework growled at me from the kitchen table, so I did my job and neutralised it. You're welcome.

133 characters. At 7:40 in the morning, Maya's maths homework was sitting on the kitchen table — and, in the dog's telling, it growled first. A guard dog does not apologise for neutralising a threat; he reports for duty and expects thanks. No cat, no paper-tasting, no "good boy" — just a dog who is completely sure he did nothing wrong. The whole joke lands in one scene: a time, a place, a named kid, and a dog treating homework like an intruder.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) checks the excuse is one line of 1–200 characters, writes it as UTF-8 with no trailing newline to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), then reads it back and verifies the bytes and the UTF-8 decode match.
